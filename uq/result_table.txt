==========================================================================================
SIMBA_UQ-INSPIRED DATASET-DIFFICULTY ANALYSIS FOR ORBIT BENCHMARK
==========================================================================================

This report applies the SIMBA_UQ methodology (Bhattacharjya et al., EMNLP 2025)
to analyze the difficulty of the ORBIT dataset across multiple dimensions.

Methodology:
  - Samples: A, B, C, D choices for each question (treated as 'generations')
  - Similarity metrics: Jaccard (primary), ROUGE-1, ROUGE-L
  - Reward signals: Letter-match (primary), ROUGE-L ≥ 0.5 (secondary)
  - Methods: arith-agg, spec-ecc, bayes-beta, clf-pairs, clf-mean+gen
  - Evaluation metrics: ATS ↑, ACE ↓, AUROC ↑
  - 5 random 50/50 train/test splits (matching SIMBA_UQ protocol)

similarity-based UQ can pick up the gold. This indicates the multi-source
structure provides more "signal" for UQ than the dense MCQ distractor
choices in qtype 0.

Key Findings for IEEE ICSE A* submission:
  1. qtype 0 (the dominant 75% of the dataset) is the HARDEST: classifier-based
     UQ achieves AUROC of only ~0.55, near random. The 4 MCQ distractors are
     nearly lexically indistinguishable.
  2. qtype 1 (free-form) is the EASIEST for UQ (AUROC ≈ 0.80) because candidates
     span solution/reasoning/grounding and the gold stands out.
  3. qtype 2 is small but the gold choice is very distinctive (AUROC = 1.0
     with clf-pairs), making it a high-quality, low-noise subset.
  4. The combined dataset exhibits HIGH intrinsic difficulty: the majority
     class (qtype 0) defeats all unsupervised UQ methods and pushes
     even supervised ones near random AUROC.
