Metric definitions (self-contained, paraphrased from SIMBA-UQ §3.2 / §4.1):
  ATS  (Accuracy from Top Selection) — fraction of items where
        argmax(confidence) coincides with the binary reward. ↑
  ACE  (Adaptive Calibration Error)  — mean |acc - conf| over K=2
        classes × B=5 equal-count adaptive bins per class. ↓
  AUROC (Area Under ROC)             — confidence vs binary reward. ↑
  All five SIMBA-UQ methods are evaluated: arith-agg, spec-ecc,
  bayes-beta, clf-pairs, clf-pairs+gen (here labelled clf-mean+gen).
  5 random 50/50 train/test splits; report mean ± std.
